Samsara playback tonight

Cavern Cinema, Thu 19 Nov 2026. Doors 6:30, film 7:00. Written 13:40 CT from a laptop feeding a JVC DLA-RS2100 projector over HDMI. Rehearse this on the real Mac, projector and speakers before doors.

What's wrong

No sound in VLC: cause not yet known. The HDMI cable is thought to run Mac → AV receiver → projector. The receiver takes the 5.1 sound off and passes the picture on. not confirmed That makes the HDMI device the right sound output. The projector has no audio out of its own [1, p.31], so the receiver has to be the device that takes the sound. The test tones in step 2 below show whether sound gets from the Mac to the speakers at all. If they play, the problem is in the player or the file.

Picture skips: the Samsara DCP is very heavy, about 233 Mbit/s most of the time, against 150 for the test DCP that plays fine. [2] DCP-o-matic Player “requires a very high-speed CPU to play DCPs in full resolution”. [3] It played fine on the Mac's own screen and skipped on the projector. The frame rate is not the problem: the DCP is 24 fps throughout. [2]

Do this first on any path: sound and display

  1. Sound out over HDMI. Apple menu → System Settings → Sound → Output: pick the HDMI device. It's usually named after the receiver. [4]
  2. Set up 5.1 and test each speaker. Open Audio MIDI Setup (Applications → Utilities) and select the HDMI device. If it offers a 6- or 8-channel format, choose it. Then click Configure Speakers → 5.1 Surround, click each speaker's Test button, and click Apply. [11] [12]
    • Tones from the right speakers: the Mac → receiver → speakers chain works.
    • No tones: check that the receiver is on the Mac's HDMI input and set to play the sound itself, not pass it to the display. The menu names depend on the receiver's model.
  3. Projector at 1080p, 24 Hz. System Settings → Displays → select the projector → Resolution 1920×1080, Refresh rate 24 Hz (48 Hz also works if 24 isn't offered). [5] The projector accepts 1080p and 4K at 24 Hz. [1, p.115] Avoid 2048×1080: the projector needs a menu change (HDMI EDID mode “B”) to accept it. [1, p.115]
  4. Plug in the charger.

Path A · the DCP in DCP-o-matic Player ready now

Folder on CS DISK: SAMSARA_FTR_F_LO-EN_51_2K_20231118_IOP_OV

  1. Open the DCP in DCP-o-matic Player.
  2. Preferences → Sound: tick Play sound and choose the HDMI device. Its mapping matrix routes the DCP's six channels to the device's outputs. It mixes 5.1 down to stereo only if the device shows just two channels, which is a sign step 2 didn't take. [6]
  3. Press play and watch the Performance area at the bottom right: it counts dropped frames. [3]
  4. If frames drop: View → Decode at half resolution, or Decode at quarter resolution if it still drops. The player suggests exactly this when it drops too many frames. [3] [7] Half resolution is slightly softer and plays smoothly.
  5. View → Full screen, or Dual screen to put the picture on the projector and the controls on the laptop. [7]

Path B · the studio's ProRes file in QuickTime

SAMSARA_EN_261114_422_5.1.mov, 89 GB, from the Filemail link the studio sent Chris on 14 Nov (it expires 14 Dec). This needs the full download on the Mac first.

  1. Open it in QuickTime Player. The M5 chip decodes ProRes in hardware, so it should play without strain. [8]
  2. The sound fix above applies here too.
  3. If you use VLC:
    • In Audio → Audio Device, pick the HDMI device. If there are several HDMI entries, try each.
    • In Audio → Audio Track, try each track. [9]
    • If it's still silent, open Window → Messages (in some versions Tools → Messages) and look for audio errors.
  4. If only one channel of sound plays, the file may store each of its six channels as a separate track, and some players play only one track. [10] We haven't seen how this file's sound is laid out.

Samsara playback tonight

Cavern Cinema, Thu 19 Nov 2026. Doors 6:30, film 7:00. Written 13:40 CT from a laptop feeding a JVC DLA-RS2100 projector over HDMI. Rehearse this on the real Mac, projector and speakers before doors.

What's wrong

No sound: the projector has no speakers and no audio out. Its manual says: “Projector has no audio pass through or eARC output.” [1, p.31] When the Mac is plugged into HDMI it can send its sound to the projector, where it goes nowhere. That is the likeliest reason VLC was silent. likely, not yet tested on the Mac

Picture skips: the Samsara DCP is very heavy, about 233 Mbit/s most of the time, against 150 for the test DCP that plays fine. [2] DCP-o-matic Player “requires a very high-speed CPU to play DCPs in full resolution”. [3] It played fine on the Mac's own screen and skipped on the projector. The frame rate is not the problem: the DCP is 24 fps throughout. [2]

Do this first on any path: sound and display

  1. Sound out to the room. Apple menu → System Settings → Sound → Output: pick the device that feeds the speakers (headphone jack, USB interface or mixer). Don't pick the projector. [4]
  2. Projector at 1080p, 24 Hz. System Settings → Displays → select the projector → Resolution 1920×1080, Refresh rate 24 Hz (48 Hz also works if 24 isn't offered). [5] The projector accepts 1080p and 4K at 24 Hz. [1, p.115] Avoid 2048×1080: the projector needs a menu change (HDMI EDID mode “B”) to accept it. [1, p.115]
  3. Plug in the charger.

Path A · the DCP in DCP-o-matic Player ready now

Folder on CS DISK: SAMSARA_FTR_F_LO-EN_51_2K_20231118_IOP_OV

  1. Open the DCP in DCP-o-matic Player.
  2. Preferences → Sound: tick Play sound and choose the room's output device. The player mixes 5.1 down to stereo by itself when the device has only two channels. [6]
  3. Press play and watch the Performance area at the bottom right: it counts dropped frames. [3]
  4. If frames drop: View → Decode at half resolution, or Decode at quarter resolution if it still drops. The player suggests exactly this when it drops too many frames. [3] [7] Half resolution is slightly softer and plays smoothly.
  5. View → Full screen, or Dual screen to put the picture on the projector and the controls on the laptop. [7]

Path B · the studio's ProRes file in QuickTime

SAMSARA_EN_261114_422_5.1.mov, 89 GB, from the Filemail link the studio sent Chris on 14 Nov (it expires 14 Dec). This needs the full download on the Mac first.

  1. Open it in QuickTime Player. The M5 chip decodes ProRes in hardware, so it should play without strain. [8]
  2. The sound fix above applies here too.
  3. If you use VLC: check Audio → Audio Device (the room system, not the projector), then Audio → Audio Track (try each track). [9] For a stereo PA, set the number of audio channels to stereo; the same Audio menu holds that setting, called “Stereo Mode” in newer VLC versions. [9]
  4. If only one channel of sound plays, the file may store each of its six channels as a separate track, and some players play only one track. [10] We haven't seen how this file's sound is laid out.
